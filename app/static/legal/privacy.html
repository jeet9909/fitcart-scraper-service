Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short: your photos are private to your account, used only to create your looks, never sold and never used for advertising. You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

This policy explains how MyDripCheck ("we", "us") handles your personal data when you use mydripcheck.com. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create and secure your account. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
Photos you uploadTo create your looks, 360° views and poses, and to keep your face true to you.
Looks you create and your wardrobeSo you can see, download and reuse them.
Product links you pasteTo fetch product name, price, sizes and images from the store.
Payment detailsHandled by Razorpay. We receive only the payment status and an order reference, never your card, UPI or bank details.
Basic usage recordsTime taken, success or failure of a look, and errors, to keep the service reliable and to fix problems.
Messages you send usTo answer your support requests.

We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell or rent your personal data.

2. Your photos

  • Photos and looks are stored in private storage. Only your account can see them, through short-lived secure links.
  • To create a look, your photo and the product images are sent to our image-generation providers (Google Gemini and, for some looks, Google Vertex AI) for processing.
  • We use a face-matching check on paid plans to make sure the face in your look matches your photo. This produces a numerical comparison used only during that look; we do not build a face database.
  • We never use your photos for advertising, and never share them publicly.

3. Who we share data with

Only with service providers that help us run MyDripCheck, each limited to what they need:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, database and private photo storage.
  • Google (Gemini API and Vertex AI): generating looks from your photo and product images.
  • Razorpay: processing payments.
  • Bright Data: fetching public product pages from store links (no personal data is sent).
  • Render and GitHub Pages: hosting our server and website.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account, photos, looks and wardrobe are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Payment records are kept as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Usage records are kept for up to 12 months.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete your account and all your photos and looks; or withdraw your consent to processing. Send your request using the contact details below. We will act on deletion requests within 30 days, and confirm when it is done.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), photos are stored privately, and access to our systems is restricted to what is needed to run the service. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as required by law.

7. Children

MyDripCheck is not meant for children under 18 without a parent or guardian. Do not upload photos of a minor without a parent's consent. If you believe a child's data has been uploaded, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you on the site or by email before the change applies.

9. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, data requests or complaints, contact the MyDripCheck grievance officer:

Email support@mydripcheck.com, or use Help & support on mydripcheck.com (bottom of every page, or in your account). We reply within 2 working days.
