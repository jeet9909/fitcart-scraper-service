Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy covers payments made to MyDripCheck for the Festival Pass and the Plus and Pro plans. Products you buy from stores such as Myntra or Amazon are refunded under that store's own refund policy.

1. What you are buying

OptionWhat you getPrice (incl. GST)
Festival Pass7 looks, valid for 7 days₹129 one time
Plus18 looks every month₹349 a month or ₹3,299 a year
Pro40 looks every month, 360° view and all poses₹799 a month or ₹7,499 a year

2. Delivery

MyDripCheck is a digital service; nothing is shipped. Your looks are added to your account immediately after a successful payment. If they do not appear within a few minutes, contact us with your payment reference and we will add them.

3. Cancellation

All passes and plans are prepaid with no automatic renewal, so there is nothing to cancel: your plan simply ends at the end of the period you paid for, and you will not be charged again unless you choose to buy again.

4. Refunds

  • Unused purchases: if you have not used any looks from a pass or plan, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of payment.
  • Failed looks: a look that fails to generate is returned to your balance automatically, so you never pay for a failed look.
  • Charged but no looks added, or charged twice: we refund the duplicate or failed payment in full.
  • Yearly plans: within 7 days of payment and with no looks used, a full refund. After that, we can refund the months not yet started, minus the months already used at the monthly price.
  • Looks that have already been used are not refundable, and unused looks from past months have no cash value.

5. How to ask for a refund

Contact us with the email on your account and your Razorpay payment reference. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach you within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

6. Contact

Email support@mydripcheck.com, or use Help & support on mydripcheck.com (bottom of every page, or in your account). We reply within 2 working days.
